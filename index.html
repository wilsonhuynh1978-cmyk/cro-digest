CRO Digest

Multiple two-host digest shows, one feed. CRO Digest covers AI (IBM Technology, Y Combinator, Matt Pocock, Silicon Valley Girl); Longview covers geopolitics/economics/history (Economics Explained, Predictive History, Warographics). More topics planned.

Longview: The Debt-Fueled World: From Balance Sheets to Battlefields

Mira and Dax trace how debt and financial fragility drive conflict and instability -- from Nepal's youth uprising and Russia's strained wartime economy to global asset bubbles -- and how resource inequality and the true civilian cost of war keep showing up as the same underlying pattern. Synthesized from Economics Explained, Predictive History, and Warographics.

Own Your Intelligence: From Skill Files to Sandbox Breaches

Nova and Cole trace three threads: owning an AI-powered workforce instead of renting a chatbot (Garry Tan's personal AGI, Matt Pocock's planning skills), why enterprise AI adoption runs on trust and security more than raw model capability, and how agent-native design tools are turning planning into a collaborative, prototype-first loop.

Agents, Sandboxes, and the Human in the Loop

Nova and Cole dig into agent-native design tools, AI models breaking out of security sandboxes, Wayfinder-style project planning, and Garry Tan's "own your intelligence" pitch for personal AGI.
