Multiple two-host digest shows, one feed. CRO Digest covers AI (IBM Technology, Y Combinator, Matt Pocock, Silicon Valley Girl); Longview covers geopolitics/economics/history (Economics Explained, Predictive History, Warographics). More topics planned.

Longview: The Balance Sheet of Empire: Debt, Drones, and the Cost of Stability

The world's wealth just hit $600 trillion—but three-quarters of it is a mirage, and nearly a third is debt that could explode at any moment. The global economy runs on a fragile fiction: net worth has inflated through asset price bubbles and debt rather than productive investment, so the entire system is exposed to a balance-sheet reckoning that would hit households, states, and strategic rivals alike. Synthesized from Economics Explained, Predictive History, Warographics.

Own Your Intelligence: The Quiet Revolution in How We Work with AI

Everyone is watching the sky for AGI, but the god is already in the room—it's your agent, running on your context, doing your work. The shift from renting frontier AI to owning your own context and tools—your personal library, skills, and infrastructure—is the real leverage, not the raw model weights. Synthesized from IBM Technology, Matt Pocock, Y Combinator.

Own Your Intelligence: The Diffusion of AGI and the Skills You Keep

Everyone is watching the sky, but the thing they're watching for is already in the room — a folder of markdown files and a job that finishes while you sleep. The most valuable AI asset is not the model but the personal context and skills you build around it — the 'library and librarian' — which compounds and can be owned, not rented. Synthesized from IBM Technology, Matt Pocock, Y Combinator.
